Our services

What we offer, and who to ask when you arrive.

Emergency care

Our emergency team assesses and treats urgent illness and injury. If you can, call before you come so we can prepare for you.

  • Urgent assessment
  • Treatment and stabilisation
  • Admission to a ward when needed

Outpatient clinics

See a doctor without being admitted. Book ahead to keep your waiting time short.

  • Consultations
  • Follow-up visits
  • Routine check-ups

Inpatient wards

Patients who need to stay are admitted to a ward with a named bed and nursing care through the day and night.

  • Admission and discharge
  • Nursing care
  • Daily review by a doctor

Laboratory

Tests ordered by your doctor are run in our laboratory and the results are added to your record.

  • Blood tests
  • Other investigations ordered by your doctor

Pharmacy

Prescriptions are filled on site, so you collect your medicine before you leave.

  • Dispensing of prescriptions
  • Advice on how to take your medicine

Maternity and women's health

Care for mothers before, during and after birth.

  • Antenatal visits
  • Delivery care
  • Postnatal follow-up

Paying for your care

We accept cash, mobile money and card, and we can send claims to your insurer. Ask the front desk to confirm which insurers we work with before your visit.

Ready to book? Send us a request and we will call you to confirm a time.
